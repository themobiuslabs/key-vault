# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers managing API keys and local developer credentials on their own machines.

## Product Purpose

KeyVault provides a simple, private desktop vault for storing developer credentials locally without a cloud account or remote server.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, trustworthy, practical.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-site polish, decorative dashboards, speculative settings, heavy visual effects, and anything that makes credential management feel less direct or less secure.

## Design Principles

- Keep security-sensitive workflows explicit and calm.
- Prefer familiar product UI patterns over novelty.
- Make local-first behavior visible through reliability, not decoration.
- Add preferences only when they support real day-to-day use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast in light and dark themes, preserve native form affordances, and respect operating-system appearance preferences.
